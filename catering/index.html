Frequently asked questions

Braai & BBQ Catering in Kent

Which areas do you cover?

Buys Braai’s provides event catering across Kent and surrounding areas. Include the venue, town or postcode in your enquiry so Oliver can confirm whether the location is suitable.

What types of events do you cater for?

Catering is available for private parties and birthdays, weddings and celebrations, corporate events and functions, festivals, markets and other public events.

Can the menu be tailored?

Yes. Menus are discussed around the occasion, venue, guest numbers, food preferences and dietary requirements rather than being limited to one fixed package.

Do you cook with gas, wood or charcoal?

Gas cooking provides a reliable and efficient option for event catering. Wood or charcoal cooking can also be considered where the venue and occasion are suitable.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form to share your date, location, event type, estimated guest numbers and food requirements. The form prepares a WhatsApp message that you can review before sending directly to Oliver.
