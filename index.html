South African-style catering in Kent

More Than a Meal Around the Fire

A braai is about good food and the people gathered around it. Buys Braai’s brings that relaxed South African-style approach to private celebrations, weddings, corporate functions, festivals and markets across Kent and surrounding areas.

Every event has different guests, timings and practical requirements. That is why the catering is tailored rather than taken from a rigid package. Gas cooking provides a reliable and efficient option for event service, with wood or charcoal cooking available where the venue and occasion are suitable.

Whether you are planning a family celebration or feeding a larger public gathering, speak directly with Oliver about what you need. The starting point is always the same: understand the event, plan around the people attending and create catering that fits.
