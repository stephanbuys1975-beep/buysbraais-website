Behind the food

Prepared for the People You’re Bringing Together

Good event catering is more than one finished plate. It begins with preparation, continues through the cook and ends with food arranged for people to share. The gallery shows each part of that journey using real Buys Braai’s photographs.

Some occasions call for slow-smoked food and time in the smoker; others suit straightforward grilling and a practical gas setup. Salads, sides, guest numbers and serving preferences are discussed as part of the wider event plan.
